Banana Bread

Description

Looking for a moist banana bread recipe that never fails? Your friends and family will ask for this never-dry banana bread again and again.

Odin Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts
  • 2 medium bananas, sliced

Steps

  1. Step 1: Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Step 2: Stir sugar and melted butter together in a large bowl. Add eggs and vanilla; mix well. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt; stir into butter mixture until smooth.
  3. Step 3: Fold in banana slices, sour cream, and walnuts; transfer into the prepared pan.
  4. Step 4: Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean, about 1 hour.
  5. Step 5: Cool loaf in the pan for 10 minutes before inverting onto a wire rack to cool completely.
  6. Step 6: Serve and enjoy!
